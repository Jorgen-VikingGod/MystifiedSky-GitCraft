# Community conduct

GitCraft welcomes contributors of all experience levels. Keep feedback specific to the work, explain requests for changes, and respect the time and privacy of other participants. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and sharing someone’s private information are grounds for moderation.

These expectations apply in GitCraft issues, pull requests, and the community Discord. For a private conduct concern in Discord, contact a project moderator privately. Use the platform’s reporting controls for abusive content on GitHub or Discord. Avoid adding private details to public issues or threads. Report product vulnerabilities through the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove disruptive content, limit participation, or refer a report to the platform. Share only the information people handling a report need.
